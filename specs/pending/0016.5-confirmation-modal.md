# The confirmation modal

## TL;DR
- `cli/components/confirm.py` holds `Confirm(ModalScreen[bool])`: the centred box, the `escape` that dismisses false, and a row with the two answers as buttons.
- `ConfirmReset` and `ConfirmStart` keep only their text, their width and their border colour.
- `ConfirmReset` gains the two buttons and loses `⏎ apagar`: the answer is chosen and then confirmed, with the safe one focused.
- This is the one subspec in [0016](../done/0016-cli-components.md) that changes what the user sees, and the change is the point.

Two confirmations that look alike and answer differently are worse than two that look different: `ConfirmReset` wipes the database on a bare `⏎`, while `ConfirmStart` ([0014](../done/0014-atividade-start-confirmation.md)) makes the student pick a button for something reversible. Part of [0016](../done/0016-cli-components.md); the reset itself is [0007](../done/0007-background-queue-and-reset.md).

## Observed behaviour
- Both are `ModalScreen[bool]` with `align: center middle` and a box of `height: auto`, `padding: 1 2`, `border: round`, `background: $surface`, differing only in width (56 and 76) and border colour (`$warning` and `$accent`).
- Both define `action_cancel` dismissing false; `ConfirmStart` adds `left` and `right` to move between the two buttons, `ConfirmReset` adds `enter` to confirm outright.
- `ConfirmReset`'s prompt spells `⏎ apagar    esc cancelar` in its own text, which [0016.1](../done/0016.1-footer-from-bindings.md) removes.
- Textual's `Button` binds `enter` to `press` with `show=False`, so a focused button already answers on `⏎` without the screen declaring anything.

## Decisions

### The destructive answer is chosen, never typed
`⏎` on the reset today is one keystroke between the command palette and an empty database. With the shared modal the student moves to an answer and presses it, and `Confirm` focuses the safe one — `Não` for the reset, `Sim` for starting a prova, since one is undoable and the other is not.

### `Confirm` owns the frame and the answers; the caller owns the text
The subclass passes a title, a body and the two labels. Building the text is where the two differ — one reads fields off an atividade, the other is a constant — and that is the half that may not move into `components/`, which cannot import `api`.

### Width and border colour are arguments
56 and 76, `$warning` and `$accent`. They are the two things that legitimately differ, so they are parameters rather than a reason to keep two modals.

## Rejected
- **Keeping `⏎` as a shortcut for yes.** It is the habit that makes the reset dangerous, and a footer entry saying `⏎ apagar` would make it look intended.
- **Extracting the frame only and leaving each modal to answer its own way.** That is today's state with fewer lines, and today's state is the problem.
- **A `notify`-style toast instead of a modal for the reset.** A toast cannot be answered, and the reset needs an answer.
