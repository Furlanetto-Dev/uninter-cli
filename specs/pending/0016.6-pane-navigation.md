# The navigation every pane repeats

## TL;DR
- `components/steps.py` gains `Steps(Widget)`, a mixin holding the `left`/`right`/`up` bindings, the three `action_step_*` methods and the `escape` that goes back.
- A pane declares where it goes as class attributes — `STEP_LEFT`, `STEP_RIGHT`, `STEP_UP`, `BACK` — instead of writing four bindings and three one-line methods.
- `AtividadeTree`, `TrabalhoTable`, `RawColumn` and `FieldsTable` become the four callers.
- The step functions themselves do not change; they are the module the mixin wraps.

`navigation/steps.py` shared the hard part — scroll first, move focus at the edge — and left every caller to write the bindings and the wrappers around it, so the same seven lines are in four modules. Part of [0016](../done/0016-cli-components.md).

## Observed behaviour
- `AtividadeTree` and `TrabalhoTable` declare the identical three bindings (`left` → `materias`, `right` → `debug`, `escape` → `back`) and the identical two `action_step_*` bodies, differing in nothing.
- `RawColumn` and `FieldsTable` declare four each, with `up` added and different destinations; `FieldsTable` replaces `action_step_up` with its own, because row zero is its edge rather than the scroll top.
- `_merge_bindings` walks the MRO base first and lets the subclass override a key, so a mixin deriving from `Widget` contributes its `BINDINGS` to `Tree`, `DataTable` and `VerticalScroll` subclasses alike, and a pane can still override one key.
- The destinations are the only difference between the four: seven action strings across four modules.

## Decisions

### Destinations are class attributes, not bindings to rewrite
`STEP_LEFT = "app.focus_materias"` says the same thing as the binding it replaces and cannot get the key or the method name wrong. A pane that does not want a direction leaves the attribute `None`, which is what `FieldsTable` does with `right` today.

### A pane may still override one step
`FieldsTable` moves the cursor up before leaving, and that is correct behaviour, not a deviation to remove. The mixin gives the default; the subclass overrides the one method it needs, which the MRO already allows.

### The mixin derives from `Widget`
Textual only merges `BINDINGS` from bases that are `DOMNode` subclasses, so a plain mixin would contribute nothing. Deriving from `Widget` puts it on the existing ancestor of all four panes and keeps the MRO linear.

## Rejected
- **A base class instead of a mixin.** The four panes already inherit from `Tree`, `DataTable` and `VerticalScroll`; only a mixin reaches all three.
- **Reading the destinations from the app.** The pane knows where it sits; a central map of who goes where would have to be kept in step with the layout by hand.
- **Folding `ctrl+c` in here.** It is [0016.7](0016.7-copyable.md)'s subject and only two of the four panes have it.
