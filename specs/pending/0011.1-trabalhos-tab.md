# Trabalhos tab

## TL;DR
- Second `TabPane#trabalhos`, "2 Trabalhos", with `TrabalhoTable`: one row per `Trabalho`, ordered by `ordem` — nome, tipo, entrega, nota, situação, prazo.
- `⏎` and `r` in this pane fetch trabalhos through the consumer of 0007; the queue item carries the collection. Not in the background sweep for now.
- `TrabalhoSyncService` is mounted in `main.py` in the same change, as 0005.1 required.
- Entregas, the devolutiva HTML, anexos and gabarito stay out of this spec.

[0005](../done/0005-trabalhos.md) left the trabalhos in the database and [0005.1](0005.1-trabalhos-na-interface.md) left them off the screen; [0011](0011-tabbed-detail-panel.md) gives them a place, and this spec fills it. Context in [CONTEXT.md](../../CONTEXT.md).

## Observed behaviour
- `Trabalho` has `nome`, `tipo`, `ordem`, `fim`, `entregue_em`, `nota`, `aprovado` (tri-state) and `pode_entregar`; the row needs nothing from `TrabalhoEntrega`. Read in `api/trabalhos/entity.py`.
- 13 of 26 matérias have no trabalho, and each sync is a three-request cascade ([0005](../done/0005-trabalhos.md), [0005.1](0005.1-trabalhos-na-interface.md)).
- `trabalhos_sincronizados_em` is a stamp of its own on `Materia` ([0005](../done/0005-trabalhos.md)), so the pane knows "never synced" without touching atividades.

## Decisions

### One row per trabalho, six columns, no entregas
Trabalho, Tipo, Entrega (`entregue_em` as `dd/mm/aa`, "aberta" while `pode_entregar`, "—" otherwise), Nota, Situação (`aprovado`: green "aprovado", red "reprovado", dim "—" when `None`), Prazo (`fim`, coloured as in [0006](../done/0006-prazo-e-cor-na-media.md)). `format_prazo`, `vencido` and `format_number` are reused. Entregas and etiquetas need a detail view, not a column.

### Fetch on demand, through the same consumer, not in the sweep
The queue item becomes (`materia_id`, collection) and the consumer dispatches to `AtividadeSyncService` or `TrabalhoSyncService`; concurrency on one `AsyncSession` is measured broken ([0007](../done/0007-fila-em-background-e-reset.md)). The sweep stays atividades-only: half the matérias would come back empty after three requests each. Revisit once the real cost is measured.

### The service is mounted with its consumer
`main.py` builds `TrabalhoSyncService` and passes it to the App in this change, as [0005.1](0005.1-trabalhos-na-interface.md) decided: no consumer, no service.

### Empty states follow the atividades pane
"⏎ para buscar no AVA" while `trabalhos_sincronizados_em` is `None`; "Esta matéria não tem trabalhos no AVA." when the fetch returned nothing. Screen text stays Portuguese ([0009](0009-english-code-portuguese-domain.md)).

### Debug shows the trabalhos capture when this pane is active
The panel of [0010](../done/0010-modo-debug.md) already renders any resource; the pane tells it which one.

## Open
- Where entregas and the devolutiva HTML go — a third tab, or a detail below the table. Decided when the table exists.
- Anexos and gabarito wait for the captures 0005.1 requires.

## Rejected
- **Trabalhos in the first sweep.** 78 extra requests, 39 of them for matérias that have none.
- **Entregas as extra columns.** A trabalho has several tentativas; the row would widen past the panel.
- **Rendering the etiqueta HTML in the row.** Over a thousand characters with `<br />` and entities ([0005.1](0005.1-trabalhos-na-interface.md)).
- **A `selected_trabalho` selection like 0010.1.** Nothing reads it yet; it comes with the detail view.
