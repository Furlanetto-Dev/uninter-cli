# The header above a detail pane

## TL;DR
- `cli/components/header.py` holds `DetailHeader(Static)` with `show(title, secondary, parts, tag=None)`: bold title, dim `·`, secondary, then the parts joined by a dim `·` on the second line.
- `MateriaHeader` becomes a caller that passes the matéria's nome, período and sync age; the prova's `tick` passes the atividade's nome, the matéria and its three state fields with the `mock` tag.
- `margin-bottom: 1` moves into the component's `DEFAULT_CSS`; the two selectors leave `app.tcss`.
- The component escapes title and secondary, which the matéria header does not do today.

The two headers are the same four elements written twice, and the second one was written by looking at the first. Part of [0016](../done/0016-cli-components.md).

## Observed behaviour
- `MateriaHeader.show` builds `[bold]{nome}[/bold] [dim]·[/dim] {período}\n[dim]{idade}[/dim]`.
- `ProvaPage.tick` builds `[bold]{nome}[/bold] [dim]·[/dim] {matéria}\n[dim]{três campos unidos por ·}[/dim] [dim]·[/dim] {mock}`, escaping both names.
- Both carry `margin-bottom: 1` under their own selector in `app.tcss`.
- The trailing tag is the only element the prova header has and the matéria header does not.

## Decisions

### The second line is a list of parts, joined by the component
Both callers hand over fields and let the header place the separators. Passing a finished string would leave the `·` in two places and let them disagree about spacing, which is the kind of difference nobody notices until the two headers are side by side.

### The tag is an optional argument, kept out of the dim run
`mock` is magenta after a dim `·`, and it is the only part that is not dim. Naming it separately keeps the second line's rule — everything dim — true for every caller.

### The component escapes what it is given
The prova header escapes because a questão can contain brackets; the matéria header does not, and a matéria whose nome contained `[` would break its own markup. Escaping in one place fixes that quietly and is the one visible difference this subspec allows.

## Rejected
- **Keeping `MateriaHeader` as a subclass.** It adds nothing but a name; the caller can query `DetailHeader` directly, as the prova screen already queries a `Static`.
- **A header that takes the entity.** It would need `Materia` and `Atividade`, and `components/` may not import `api` ([0016](../done/0016-cli-components.md)).
