# The presencial mark on an atividade

## TL;DR
- `Atividade.presencial` comes from `avaliacao.avaliacaoPresencial`, through `ProvaDTO`.
- The tree shows `presencial` in its own colour right after the name of an atividade that is one; nothing for the others.
- Ships with [0013](0013-materia-groups.md), whose start-up schema reset adds the column to an existing database.

Knowing which provas are taken in person changes how you plan the week, and the AVA already says it. Context in [CONTEXT.md](../../CONTEXT.md); the tree in [0012](0012-grouped-atividades.md).

## Observed behaviour
- In the captured atividades responses, all 151 items carry `avaliacaoPresencial` inside `avaliacao`, never at the top level. 84 are true: every Prova Objetiva and Prova Discursiva, whatever the variant. 67 are false: APOLs, Atividade Prática and simulados.

## Decisions

### A boolean on the entity, read from the nested `avaliacao`
`presencial: bool = False` on `Atividade`; `ProvaDTO.presencial` with alias `avaliacaoPresencial`, carried by `to_atividade`. Portuguese field, like the rest.

### A word after the name, not a column or an icon
The leaf label appends ` presencial` in magenta after the name when the flag is true. It counts toward the name column width, so the columns stay aligned. A word reads at a glance and needs no legend; an icon would.

## Rejected
- **A seventh column.** Six columns already fill a 100-column pane; a boolean does not earn one.
- **An icon such as ⌂.** Needs a legend, and one more glyph among `▼`, `├──` and `…`.
- **Printing `online` on the others.** Doubles the noise to say the default.

## What execution corrected
- The tag counts toward the name column width through `_nome_width`, so a presencial prova with a long name is truncated a little earlier instead of pushing the other columns.
- Verified headless together with 0012's flow: the regular prova carries `presencial` in its label and the APOL does not.
