# Into the debug panel, and out with the JSON

## TL;DR
- `→` on the selected atividade moves focus into the open debug panel; `←` on its left column, or `esc` anywhere in it, comes back to the table. `Enter` stays free for a future action.
- The panel becomes two focusable columns, AVA and SQLite: `←`/`→` switch, `↑`/`↓` scroll the focused one.
- `ctrl+c copy` in the footer copies the focused column as JSON, through the terminal (OSC 52).
- The focus chain is list → table → panel, one step per `→`, one back per `←`.

[0010.1](0010.1-selectable-atividades.md) narrowed the panel to one atividade, but reaching it still takes `tab`, reading a 160-field item scrolls both columns in lockstep, and getting the JSON out means redoing a curl. Context in [CONTEXT.md](../../CONTEXT.md); the panel in [0010](0010-modo-debug.md); names in [0009](../pending/0009-english-code-portuguese-domain.md).

## Observed behaviour
- Textual 8.2.8 `App.copy_to_clipboard(text)` writes an OSC 52 sequence to the terminal, and its docstring says macOS Terminal ignores it. Checked in the source.
- Textual binds `ctrl+c` to `help_quit`, a notice that it no longer quits, and reserves the key for copy in `Input` and `TextArea`. Checked in `App.BINDINGS`.
- Inference, not measured: the screenshots show Alacritty inside tmux. Alacritty accepts OSC 52; tmux forwards it only with `set -g set-clipboard on`.
- Since 0010.1 the panel shows a single object, so the row alignment that justified one shared scroll in 0010 no longer applies.

## Decisions

### Entering the panel is `→` from the table, and only from the table
`AtividadeTable` binds `right` to `app.focus_debug`, which focuses the left column when the panel is open and does nothing otherwise: `j` opens, `→` enters. The list still goes through the table, so a matéria with no rows cannot enter the panel; its data stays visible without focus.

### The panel is two focusable columns, each with its own scroll
`DebugPanel` becomes a meta line over a `Horizontal` of two `VerticalScroll` columns, `RawColumn` (AVA) and `StoredColumn` (SQLite). The focused column carries the highlight. `←`/`→` move between them; `↑`/`↓`/`PgUp`/`PgDn` scroll the focused one. Independent scroll is fine now that each side holds one object. `←` on the left column returns to the table; `esc` returns to the table from either column; `→` on the right column does nothing.

### `ctrl+c` copies the focused column as JSON
The binding lives on the columns, so `ctrl+c copy` shows in the footer while one has focus — outside the panel there is no JSON under the cursor. The raw column copies the item as the AVA sent it, `json.dumps(item, indent=2, ensure_ascii=False)`; the stored column copies `model_dump_json(indent=2)`. It goes through `App.copy_to_clipboard`, no new dependency, and a notification confirms. Textual's own `ctrl+c` notice is replaced by the binding.

### What the panel shows does not change
Entering and leaving only move focus. The panel keeps following the selection as in 0010.1, and `d home` works from inside it.

## Rejected
- **`tab` as the way in.** Works today, but is invisible and ignores the list → table → panel order.
- **`Enter` as a second way in.** `Enter` on an atividade is reserved for a future action; `→` is the one way in, and `j` the one switch that opens the panel.
- **One shared scroll with a highlighted column.** Simpler, but `↑`/`↓` could not scroll one side past the other, and the alignment it preserved is gone since 0010.1.
- **An always-visible `ctrl+c` copying "what is under the cursor".** Would need a meaning on the list and on the table, where no JSON is on screen.
- **`pyperclip` or an `xclip` subprocess.** A dependency to do what the terminal already does; revisit only if OSC 52 fails in the user's terminal.
- **Copying both columns at once.** Two documents in one paste; the user picks a side with `←`/`→`.

## What execution corrected
- Under pyright strict a widget's `self.app` is `App[Unknown]`, so the column posts a `Copy` message and the App calls `copy_to_clipboard`; the column only notifies.
- `→` on the right column needs no binding: `VerticalScroll` hides horizontal overflow, so its own `scroll_right` is already a no-op.
- `←` and `esc` fall back to the list when the table has no rows, so focus never lands on a hidden widget after a refetch that emptied the matéria.
- The old `esc voltar` label on the panel became `esc back`, in line with the English footer of 0010.1.
- Verified headless: `→` into the panel only when open, `ctrl+c` copying each column's JSON, `←`/`→` between columns, `esc` from either, `j` closing back to the table, `d` from inside, and a 404 matéria keeping `→` on the list.
- The focus highlight is a round border, `$panel` when blurred and `$border` blue when focused, with the column name as border title, since the 10% tint alone was invisible in practice.
- The SQLite column became a `DataTable` at the user's request: zebra rows, fixed field column, and a row cursor that ↑/↓ move. The table itself takes the focus, so the column's keys (`ctrl+c`, `esc`, `←`) live on it and the border follows `:focus-within`. `AtividadeTable` now translates its own `RowHighlighted` into a typed `Selected` message, because the fields table's highlights bubbled into the same handler and cleared the selection.
- `←`/`→` first scroll a panel that overflows horizontally and switch panels only on a press made while already at that edge, so holding the key never overshoots into the neighbour. Applied to both debug columns and to the atividades table; `cli/navigation/sideways.py` holds the two steps.
