# The debug panel stops hiding the atividades on short terminals

## TL;DR
- Under 40 rows, `Screen.-debug.-short` no longer hides `#collections`; it caps `AtividadeTable` at its header plus five rows, and the table scrolls to the cursor.
- In the same state the vertical spacing of `#detail`, `MateriaHeader`, `DebugPanel` and `#debug-meta` goes to zero, and the meta line stops wrapping.
- The breakpoint stays at 40 rows and `-tall` does not change.

[0010](../done/0010-debug-mode.md) decided that on a short terminal the panel replaces the atividades table, when the panel still listed every atividade. Since [0010.1](../done/0010.1-selectable-atividades.md) it shows the one atividade selected in that table, so hiding the table hides the only way to choose. Context in [CONTEXT.md](../../CONTEXT.md); the tabs and `#collections` in [0011](../done/0011-tabbed-detail-panel.md).

## Observed behaviour
Measured headless on a copy of `uninter.db`, 160 columns, the matéria with ten atividades.
- At 24, 30 and 36 rows with debug on, `#collections` has `display: none`, yet `→` from the list focuses the table, `↓` moves the selection (cursor on row 9, table region 0 rows high) and the panel redraws for an atividade nobody can see. Focusing a hidden widget succeeds silently, as 0011 found.
- The two captured URLs are 112 and 138 characters. In a 112-column detail area the two-line meta takes six rows, seven with its margin; `text-wrap: nowrap` brings it to two, one once an atividade is selected.
- With the table capped at six rows, the panel columns keep 1 row at 24 rows and 4 at 30. Trimming four rows of spacing and the wrap gives 7 and 13.
- `height: auto` under the cap keeps a five-atividade matéria at six rows and a matéria without atividades at zero, where `→` stays on the list as before.

| terminal rows | today (table hidden) | capped | capped, trimmed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 24 | 10 | 1 | 7 |
| 30 | 16 | 4 | 13 |
| 36 | 22 | 10 | 19 |

Rows left for the panel's two columns.

## Decisions

### `-short` caps the table instead of hiding the collections
The `display: none` on `#collections` becomes `max-height: 6` on `AtividadeTable`, under the same `Screen.-debug.-short` selector. `DataTable` is `height: auto; max-height: 100%`, so the cap bounds it and its own cursor scrolling keeps the selected row in view; a shorter matéria stays shorter. Five rows show the selection and its neighbours, which is all choosing needs, and the 160-field JSON gets the rest.

### With the panel open on a short terminal, spacing and wrapping go
Under `Screen.-debug.-short`: `#detail` padding `0 2`, `MateriaHeader` and `DebugPanel` margins 0, and `#debug-meta` with margin 0, `text-wrap: nowrap` and `text-overflow: ellipsis`. It is what turns a one-row panel at 24 rows into a seven-row one, and it touches only the state where rows are scarce, so the normal look does not move.

### The breakpoint stays at 40, and `-tall` is untouched
Above 40 the whole table fits with 15 rows of panel, as 0010 computed. Only the meaning of `-short` changes: from "the panel replaces the table" to "the table shrinks to a window around the cursor".

## Rejected
- **`#collections` and the panel at `1fr` each.** Measured: at 36 rows the table gets nine rows the cursor does not need and the panel loses three; at 24 the table shows two rows and the panel one.
- **A percentage `max-height` on the table.** It resolves against the auto-height pane and the table sticks at two rows whatever the terminal.
- **Table while choosing, panel while reading, swapped by focus.** Every `↓` is a blind step again; following the cursor is what the panel is for.
- **Only stopping `focus_pane` from focusing the hidden table.** Removes the blind cursor and leaves no way to pick an atividade under 40 rows.
- **Hiding the média in `-short`.** Three rows, but it is the aggregate one checks the atividades against, and its inline `display` (0010) means code, not CSS.
- **Hiding the tab strip in `-short`.** Two rows, but 0011 made it the panel's title and the digit hint.
- **Lowering the breakpoint.** The table does not fit whole under 40 rows; the number was right, the rule under it was wrong.

## What execution corrected
- Between the spec and the fix, [0012](../done/0012-grouped-atividades.md) turned the table into `AtividadeTree` with `AtividadeHeader` as a row of its own, and [0011.1](../done/0011.1-trabalhos-tab.md) added the trabalhos pane, whose selection the panel also follows. The cap sits on `AtividadeTree` and `TrabalhoTable`; with the header outside the tree the atividades pane takes seven rows, one more than the table did, so the panel keeps 6, 12 and 18 rows at 24, 30 and 36.
- Verified headless on a copy of `uninter.db` at 160 columns: `Tree` keeps its cursor line in view under the cap on its own, `→` enters the panel and `esc` comes back at 24, 30 and 36 rows, `2` shows a capped trabalhos table, a matéria without atividades keeps `→` on the list, turning debug off restores the padding, and 40 and 44 rows render exactly as before.
