# Entregar trabalhos

We need to send the entrega of a trabalho from the app, so that finishing one does not end in the browser.

Today the app only reads trabalhos: it mirrors the etapas, the entregas and the correção, and writes nothing back.

The AVA asks for three things at once: where the atividade prática was done, chosen from a fixed list (no polo, no seu ambiente de trabalho, com recursos próprios, em laboratório virtual, outros ambientes, em ambiente prático/profissional, na comunidade, no polo + laboratório virtual); a comentário in free text; and a single .rar or .zip of up to 200 MB. The prazo sits above them and one button sends the three together.

Whether the student names a file already on disk or chooses one from inside the app is open, and so is what a second entrega does to the first.
